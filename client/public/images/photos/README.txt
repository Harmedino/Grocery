Real photos for the website go in this folder (JPG or WebP, about 1200px wide).

Then set the matching path in client/src/config/store.js -> PHOTOS, e.g.
  hero: "/images/photos/hero.jpg"

Slots (any slot left empty shows an illustration instead):
  hero           top of the home page: a customer with her groceries, or the shop
  payOnDelivery  a customer paying the delivery rider at her door
  stepChoose     someone ordering on their phone
  stepPack       staff packing an order
  stepDeliver    the delivery rider on the way
  stepPay        paying at the door
  shop           the shop front or inside (About page)
